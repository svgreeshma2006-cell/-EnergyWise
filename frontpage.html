The Challenge

Why Electricity Awareness Matters

Consumers face invisible barriers that lead to unnecessarily high utility bills and wasted energy — every single month.

🔍
Hidden High Consumers
Most households have no idea which appliances are silently draining the most electricity. Identifying the culprits requires expert-level analysis.
💰
Complex Tariff Structures
Electricity tariff slabs, Time-of-Day pricing, and BEE Star ratings are opaque to everyday consumers. Most people overpay without realising it.
👻
Phantom / Standby Loads
Devices in standby mode silently consume 5–10% of total electricity. This "vampire drain" costs ₹400–800/month with zero benefit.
⏰
Peak Hour Blindness
Running heavy appliances during peak hours (6–9 AM, 5–9 PM) costs up to 20% more on ToD tariffs. Most consumers never shift loads.
📊
No Real-Time Insights
Without personalised, real-time guidance, consumers receive their bill shocked — with no actionable path to reduce it next month.
🌍
Carbon Impact Invisible
Every kWh emits 0.82 kg CO₂ in India's grid. Without tangible analogies, consumers feel no connection between their usage and climate impact.
